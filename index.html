Projects

PharmaShelf — Pharmacy Inventory & Management System

Personal Project

PharmaShelf is a comprehensive, offline-first Android application designed for retail pharmacies, clinical store managers, and pharmacists to streamline medicine inventory, stock registration, expiry monitoring, prescription analysis, and point-of-sale billing.

Key Features

  • Manual medicine registration with medicine name, batch ID, expiry date, quantity, cost price, category, and shelf rack location.
  • Photo-assisted medicine registration using AI vision analysis to extract batch numbers, expiry dates, and dosage details from medicine packaging or strips.
  • Search and filtering of inventory by brand name, batch number, category, and storage rack location.
  • Stock status indicators for healthy stock, low-stock items, and critical shortages.
  • Expiry monitoring with categories for medicines expiring within 30 days, 60 days, and already expired medicines.
  • Batch-level traceability to support safe dispensing and timely disposal of expired medicines.
  • Point-of-sale billing with cart management, quantity adjustment, discounts, and tax calculation.
  • Automatic stock deduction after completing a sale.
  • AI-powered prescription analysis to identify prescribed medicines, dosages, and administration instructions from prescription images.
  • Cross-referencing prescription medicines with medicines available in the pharmacy inventory.
  • Offline local data persistence using the Room database without requiring continuous internet connectivity.

Technologies Used

  • Room Database
